What is RocketGPT?

RocketGPT is a browser-based AI chatbot for everyday questions, writing help, project planning, and creative work. It uses the GPT-OSS model through Pollinations' free text service. Replies are generated by the AI service; they may be inaccurate and should be checked when the details matter.

The anonymous free service is best-effort and rate-limited to one reply per public IP address every 30 seconds. Availability and limits are controlled by the provider. Chat messages are sent to that provider to generate responses, so do not enter passwords, payment information, or other sensitive details. Chat history is saved in this browser only.

Frequently asked questions

Is RocketGPT free to use?

RocketGPT uses the provider's anonymous free AI service. It requires no account or API key, but has a shared per-IP rate limit and may occasionally be unavailable.

Do I need an OpenAI key?

No. RocketGPT uses GPT-OSS through Pollinations, not the OpenAI API.

Can RocketGPT browse the web?

No. The current free chat does not have web search, so it cannot verify live news or current webpages.
