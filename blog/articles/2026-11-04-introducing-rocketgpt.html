Introducing RocketGPT

RocketGPT is the company behind Rocket 2.0 and NALL-E, focused on building and bringing practical AI products to people.

The homepage now explains how the RocketGPT brand relates to its products. Rocket 2.0 is a browser-based AI creative workspace, and NALL-E is a text-to-image experience.

The products currently use third-party models and services rather than models trained from scratch by RocketGPT. We aim to identify those providers where they are used.
