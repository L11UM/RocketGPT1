A more reliable Rocket 2.0 chat

Rocket 2.0 now gives a clearer path through slow or failed responses, including a retry option that keeps your unsent message available.

Chat requests have a timeout, and when a request fails, Rocket keeps the message in the composer so it can be retried. Failed turns are not added to the conversation context.

The AI provider still controls response quality and availability. These changes make failures easier to recover from, but cannot guarantee that an external service is always online.
